Intro

I’m Faisal Chowdhury Galib, a Statistics graduate and currently a Visiting Instructor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of North Florida (UNF). My interests include statistical modeling, machine learning, biostatistics, public health, and applied data science, with a focus on using data-driven methods to address real-world problems.

I have more than two years of teaching experience through my roles as a Visiting Instructor, Graduate Teaching Assistant, and SMART Center Tutor at UNF. My teaching experience includes mathematics and statistics instruction, leading recitation sessions, providing individualized academic support, and helping students develop strong quantitative and problem-solving skills.

My research and data science experience includes working with the International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (icddr,b), where I contributed to public health and environmental health research, as well as serving as a Data Science Intern at Florida Data Science for Social Good (FL-DSSG). Through these experiences, I have worked with real-world datasets involving data cleaning, statistical analysis, machine learning, visualization, geospatial analysis, and research communication.

I enjoy combining statistics, research, programming, and data visualization to transform complex data into meaningful insights. Explore my projects to learn more about my work in statistical research, data analytics, machine learning, and applied data science.
